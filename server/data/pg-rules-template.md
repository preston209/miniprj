# PG Resident Rulebook & Policy Guide

## Section 1: Security, Entry & Curfew Policy
- All main entrances are secured 24/7 via biometric access and corridor CCTV.
- Standard quiet curfew begins at 11:30 PM. Residents returning late from work shifts or travel can submit a digital late-entry pass or inform the night caretaker.
- Quiet hours are observed from 11:00 PM to 6:30 AM across residential floors.

## Section 2: Security Deposit, Lock-In & Notice Period
- A refundable security deposit is collected prior to move-in.
- Standard lock-in period is 2 months for shared rooms and 1 month for single studios.
- Residents must submit a 30-day prior written notice in the Resident Portal before checkout.
- 100% of the refundable deposit (less pending electricity sub-meter charges or documented damages) is refunded within 7 working days via bank transfer/UPI.

## Section 3: Visitors & Guest Policy
- Day visitors are welcome between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM in the ground-floor common lounge after signing the security log with government photo ID.
- Overnight guests are not permitted in shared rooms. Single-room residents may request 48-hour advance written approval from property management.

## Section 4: Electricity Sub-Meter & Rent Billing
- Monthly rent invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and due by the 5th.
- Each room has an individual digital sub-meter billed at ₹9 per unit, split equally among occupied beds in shared rooms.
- Long-term residential stays qualify for 0% GST and HRA-compliant downloadable rent receipts.

## Section 5: Conduct, Housekeeping & Meals
- Smoking, vaping, alcohol, and banned substances are strictly prohibited anywhere on the property.
- Room cleaning occurs 3 times per week, and bed linen is refreshed weekly.
- Meal opt-outs must be toggled at least 4 hours prior to meal service to minimize food waste.
